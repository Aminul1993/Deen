Today's prices

Use today's local rate for the kind of gold you own (such as 22 carat), for example from your jewellers' association.

What you own

Include jewellery you wear: in the Hanafi school it is zakatable. Gems such as diamonds aren't, unless kept for sale.

Loans you've given that you expect to be repaid. You may pay their zakat when you get them back.

Stock you bought to sell, at today's selling price.

At today's value. If you hold shares for their dividends rather than to sell, ask a scholar: only part may be zakatable.

What you owe

Money you owe now, and this year's instalments of long-term loans such as a home loan.

Your zakat

  • Zakat is due on wealth at or above the nisab once you have held it for a full lunar (Hijri) year.
  • Your home, car, household goods and personal belongings aren't zakatable.
  • This follows the Hanafi school. For a business, shares, loans or other complex cases, ask a qualified Hanafi scholar.
